# Agentic AI Security CMM — Standards Crosswalk Archive

The former standards matrix was written for an earlier CMM with a scored L5+ tier and numeric dependency caps. It is preserved in the repository as a [point-in-time archive](../../docs/aai-s-cmm-standards-crosswalk-archive-2026-09.md); its mappings and maturity statements do not govern a current assessment.

Use the [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-2026|current CMM]] and [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-measurement-protocol|Assessor's Handbook]] for criterion and evidence decisions. The [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-crosswalk-canada-fi|Canadian]] and [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-crosswalk-us-fi|US]] crosswalks address jurisdiction-specific evidence reuse. An external standard clause must be checked against its applicable edition and the deployment before it supports a finding.
