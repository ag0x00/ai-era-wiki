# Entities Index

People, organizations, products. Three subfolders:

- [[wiki/entities/organizations/_index|Organizations]] — companies, standards bodies, consortia
- [[wiki/entities/people/_index|People]] — researchers, practitioners, authors
- [[wiki/entities/products/_index|Products]] — vendor tools, platforms, model offerings

## Convention

Standards bodies / consortia (NIST, OWASP, MITRE, ISO, CoSAI) are organizations. The frameworks they publish (NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS, OWASP LLM Top 10) get separate pages in `wiki/frameworks/` that link back here.
