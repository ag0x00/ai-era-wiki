# Kyle Polley

Author and maintainer of [[redai|RedAI]], the open-source AI vulnerability-discovery workbench whose distinguishing architectural commitment is **live-environment validation as a plugin**: scanner agents produce candidates, validator agents prove or disprove them inside a running instance of the target (Chrome, iOS Simulator, or any plugin a user implements).
