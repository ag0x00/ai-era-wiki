# John Sotiropoulos

Co-lead of the OWASP Agentic Security Initiative (ASI), the stream within the [[owasp|OWASP GenAI Security Project]] that produces the project's agentic-AI security guidance.[^ack] He shares the ASI co-lead role with Ron F. Del Rosario (SAP).[^ack] His affiliations on OWASP material are ETSI and Deep Cyber.[^ack]

The ASI working group he co-leads is the source of the wiki's core OWASP agentic references: the [[owasp-agentic-ai-top-10|Top 10 for Agentic Applications (ASI Top 10)]], the [[owasp-agentic-ai-threats-mitigations|Agentic AI Threats and Mitigations]] guide, and the [[owasp-state-of-agentic-ai-security-governance|State of Agentic AI Security and Governance]] report.

## Relevance to the Wiki

Sotiropoulos is named on the contributor roster of the v2 [[owasp-state-of-agentic-ai-security-governance|State of Agentic AI Security and Governance]] report (June 2026) in his ASI co-lead capacity.[^ack] He is a standards-side anchor for the OWASP agentic taxonomy that the wiki's [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-2026|Capability Maturity Model]] and [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-crosswalk|crosswalk]] map against.

> The report's three named section co-leads are Ariel Fogel (Pillar Security), Rock Lambros (Zenity / RockCyber), and Evgeniy Kokuykin (HiveTrace), not the ASI initiative co-leads; the document is collectively authored by the OWASP GenAI Security Project rather than attributed to a single lead author.[^ack]

Del Rosario, ASI co-lead, SAP"; section co-leads listed as Ariel Fogel, Rock Lambros, and Evgeniy Kokuykin. `.raw/papers/owasp-state-of-agentic-ai-security-governance-v2.01.pdf`.
