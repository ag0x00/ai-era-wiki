# Nahum Korda

**Sources:** [Knostic blog — *Introducing OpenAnt*](https://www.knostic.ai/blog/openant) (Research credit).

## Identity

Research lead on [[openant|OpenAnt]] at [[knostic|Knostic]]. Bylined as the research lead in the *Credit* section of [[openant-announcement|the OpenAnt announcement]]; productization credited separately to Alex Raihelgaus and Daniel Geyshis.

## Relevance to This Wiki

Lead researcher behind [[adversarial-reflexion|Adversarial Reflexion]] — the load-bearing technique that lets OpenAnt produce verified-exploitable findings at OSS-maintainer scale (5-orders-of-magnitude candidate reduction at ~\$443 / OpenSSL token cost) by removing the agreeable-judge false-positive class at the architecture level rather than at the prompt-engineering level. The technique is the wiki's second-domain confirmation (alongside [[mdash-defense-at-ai-speed|MDASH]] and [[xbow-mythos-evaluation|XBOW × Mythos]]) of *FP-control as architectural primary* in AI vulnerability discovery.
