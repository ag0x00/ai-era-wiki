# Maxim Kovalsky

Practitioner at [[consortium-networks|Consortium Networks]], a value-added reseller (VAR). His prior work spans the federal intelligence community (cybercrime investigations), early-stage cyber startups, a Big Four firm, and industry leadership roles.

At [[unprompted-conference-march-2026|Unprompted March 2026]] he presented [[ai-security-larsen-effect-talk|"The AI Security Larsen Effect"]], naming the vendor-marketing feedback loop that swamps buyer-side signal and demonstrating an agent-built capability-evaluation tool that resolves each AI-security capability into a configure, buy, or build decision against the actual deployment.
