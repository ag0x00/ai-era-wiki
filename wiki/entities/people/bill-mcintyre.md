# Bill McIntyre

Author of the **"Securing Your Agents — Approaches to Agentic Dev Security"** slide deck (40 slides, 2026), released under GPL v3.0. The deck is a practitioner-oriented synthesis of OWASP LLM Top 10, OWASP Agentic Top 10 / ASI, the Lethal Trifecta, and Johann Rehberger's "Month of AI Bugs" case studies, packaged as a layered defense playbook.

Affiliated with the AI/ML Engineering (AIE) subgroup of RMAIIG (presumed to be the Rocky Mountain AI Interest Group based on naming conventions; not independently verified).

- [[securing-your-agents-talk|Securing Your Agents]] (2026) — six-section layered-defense playbook covering threat model, input sanitization, prompt hardening, output / action constraints, infrastructure, and red-teaming.
