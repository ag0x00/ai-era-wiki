# Peter Smith

Director, Agentic SOC Product Management at [[salesforce|Salesforce]]. Co-presented [[beyond-the-chatbot-talk|Beyond the Chatbot — Delivering an Agentic SOC for Real-World Defense]] with [[ravi-kiran-sharma|Ravi Kiran Sharma]] at [[unprompted-conference-march-2026|Unprompted Conference]] (March 4, 2026, Day 2 / Stage 1 / 15:05).

## Contribution to this wiki

Smith is the product-management lead for the Salesforce Agentic SOC. The talk argues for a Polyphonic (Supervisor-Worker) architecture as the successor to monolithic SOC copilots. See [[beyond-the-chatbot-talk|the talk page]] for the published abstract; slides and transcript are not yet captured.
