# Wendy Nather

**Sources:** [[mythos-ready-briefing|Mythos-ready paper]] (cited inline as creator of *Cyber Poverty Line*).

## Identity

Cybersecurity strategist and analyst, creator of the **[[cyber-poverty-line|Cyber Poverty Line]]** concept describing the structural capacity gap that separates organizations able to mount adequate self-defense from those that cannot. The concept predates the Mythos era and has been load-bearing in cybersecurity-economics discourse for years.

## Relevance to This Wiki

The Cyber Poverty Line is the wiki's anchor for the *Mythos-era widens the capacity gap; collective defense is required* argument. Surfaced in the [[mythos-ready-briefing|Mythos-ready strategic briefing]]'s §II "Build Collective Defense Now":

> *"Engage now with sector coordinating groups, ISACs, CERTs, and standards bodies to share threat intelligence, coordinate response, and produce sector-specific guidance for this moment. Defenders must do the same and leverage our coordinating groups, especially when considering organizations that fall below the Cyber Poverty Line, as introduced by Wendy Nather."*
