# Ken Huang

Ken Huang is named on the [[owasp-aivss|OWASP AIVSS]] leadership team and is the project lead of the [[owasp-agentic-skills-top-10|OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10]] (AST10), where Fabio Cerullo is project co-leader.[^aivss][^ast10] No source names a pronoun for Huang; this page uses they/them.

## Relevance to the Wiki

The two credits rest on different evidence. The AIVSS credit has a primary source: the AIVSS v0.8 specification thanks "the AIVSS leadership team—Ken Huang, Michael Bargury, Vineeth Sai Narajala, Bhavya Gupta, and Tim Marple", lists the same five under **Leader Authors**, and carries "Ken Huang, OWASP" in its founding-member list.[^aivss] The specification names a team of five and no single lead, so the table row on [[agentic-ai-security-cmm-2026|the 2026 capability maturity model]] reading "OWASP AIVSS lead" states the credit more narrowly than the document supports.

The AST10 project-lead credit rests on the project page alone. This page records it as that page's own statement, retrieved 2026-09-16, and no second, independent source corroborates it here.[^ast10]

Huang is also credited as an author on the [[precize-agentic-ai-top10|Precize Agentic AI Top 10]] and named, alongside Vishwas Manral and Akram Sheriff, on that initiative's AI & Cloud Governance Council landing page as a founder and key contributor.[^precize]

[^ast10]: OWASP, [Agentic Skills Top 10 (AST10) project page](https://owasp.org/www-project-agentic-skills-top-10/), retrieved 2026-09-16. Names Ken Huang and Fabio Cerullo as project leads. Not independently corroborated by a second source.
