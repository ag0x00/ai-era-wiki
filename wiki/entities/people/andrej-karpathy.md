# Andrej Karpathy

**Sources:** [Homepage](https://karpathy.ai) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Karpathy)

Andrej Karpathy is a computer scientist, an OpenAI co-founder, and the former Director of AI at Tesla.

On this wiki he is the originator of [[vibe-coding|vibe coding]], a term he coined in a February 2025 post on X to describe generating and running code by describing intent in natural language and "giving in to the vibes" for quick, throw-away projects. His earlier "Software 2.0" framing anticipated the shift the wiki tracks under [[citizen-coders|Citizen Coders]]. The autoresearch pattern this vault's research loop is modeled on also traces to his work.
