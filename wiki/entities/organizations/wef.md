# World Economic Forum (WEF)

**Sources:** [World Economic Forum (homepage)](https://www.weforum.org) · [Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026 (PDF)](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Global_Cybersecurity_Outlook_2026.pdf)

International organization publishing the annual **Global Cybersecurity Outlook** — a senior-leader survey with disclosed methodology that complements IBM/Ponemon cost framing on the wiki.

## Notable outputs

- **Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026** ([reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Global_Cybersecurity_Outlook_2026.pdf](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Global_Cybersecurity_Outlook_2026.pdf)) — Headline findings: 87% identify AI vulnerabilities as fastest-growing cyber risk; 94% say AI is the dominant change driver; ~33% lack any AI pre-deployment security review. Annual; senior-leader respondent base; methodology disclosed.

## Relevance to this corpus

WEF GCO is the canonical "what senior leaders think about cyber risk" survey at international scale. For a wiki citing IBM cost data, WEF provides the matching *executive perception* dimension — useful for D1 (Governance & Accountability) and D9 (Operations & Human Factors) CMM evidence at L4+.
