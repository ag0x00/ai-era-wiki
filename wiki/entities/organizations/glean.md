# Glean

**Sources:** [Glean (homepage)](https://www.glean.com) · [Glean security](https://www.glean.com/security)

> [!gap] Stub
> Enterprise AI search platform. Vector-based search across SaaS connectors (M365, Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, Zendesk, Jira, etc.) plus internal documents.
>
> Frequently named alongside Microsoft Copilot in 2026 enterprise AI security discussions because both can drive [[oversharing-controls|oversharing]]. The Knostic article cites Glean specifically: vector search can overshare by combining permissible but contextually inappropriate content. Knostic positions itself as a knowledge-layer governance overlay for Glean (and Copilot, Gemini).
>
> Pending: founding/funding context, product architecture, security posture and certifications, key customers, competitive positioning vs Microsoft Copilot.
