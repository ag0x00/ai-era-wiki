# Consortium Networks

[Consortium Networks](https://consortium.net) is a cybersecurity value-added reseller (VAR) that matches client requirements to security products in the marketplace. In the wiki it appears through [[maxim-kovalsky|Maxim Kovalsky]]'s [[ai-security-larsen-effect-talk|"AI Security Larsen Effect"]] talk at [[unprompted-conference-march-2026|Unprompted March 2026]].

Consortium maintains an agent-built, continuously refreshed database of AI-security vendor claims (thousands of claims across roughly 80 vendors as of the talk), each claim assigned an evidence-backed confidence rating and mapped to a custom AI risk taxonomy synthesized from the [[owasp-llm-top-10|OWASP LLM Top 10]], the [[nist-ai-rmf|NIST AI RMF]], and [[mitre-atlas|MITRE ATLAS]]. A nightly [[automated-prompt-optimization|agentic workflow]] re-researches vendors so each is revisited at least monthly.
