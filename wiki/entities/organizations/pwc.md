# PwC

**Sources:** [PwC × Anthropic alliance](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/technology/alliances/anthropic.html) · [Claude partner blog](https://claude.com/blog/how-our-partners-are-putting-opus-to-work-for-cybersecurity)

## Identity and role

Global professional-services firm. Its **Claude Native Cybersecurity** offering pairs *Secure AI Adoption* — moving enterprises from sandbox to production in weeks, with governance and audit evidence — with *Scaled Frontier Defense*, which folds [[mythos|Claude Opus]]-powered agentic reasoning into vulnerability-management, detection, security-engineering, and GRC workflows under defined guardrails. PwC also publishes the [[pwc-stage-coverage-tiers|stage-coverage maturity tiers]] for agentic SDLC.

## Relevance to This Wiki
PwC is the governed-AI-into-production entry in the [[claude-partners-opus-cybersecurity|Opus partner ecosystem]], targeting "pilot purgatory" — the controls, audit evidence, and autonomy boundaries that keep security AI stuck in pilots.
