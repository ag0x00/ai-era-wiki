# Taiwan Ministry of Digital Affairs

Cabinet-level ministry responsible for Taiwan's digital governance and cybersecurity policy. Its National Institute of Cyber Security operates the monitoring units that detect and respond to intrusions against government agencies.

## Relevance to This Wiki

The ministry's cybersecurity monitoring units detected an "abnormal attack" against government agencies in July 2026 and, beginning July 20, issued a series of warning alerts while investigating. Its public statement, on 2026-08-13, confirmed the attack showed "clear characteristics of an overseas source" combining manual operations with AI agent-assisted attacks, naming OpenClaw specifically, and that "the affected units have successively completed their handling."[^reuters] It did not name China as the source; China's Taiwan Affairs Office did not respond to Reuters' request for comment.

The statement is the first instance in this wiki of a national government issuing a same-week public confirmation of an AI-agent-driven intrusion against its own agencies, one day after the technical account was shared with the press by the researching vendor, [[dream-security|Dream Security]].
