# Qualys

**Sources:** [Qualys (homepage)](https://www.qualys.com) · [Enterprise Patch & Remediation Benchmark 2026](https://blog.qualys.com/qualys-insights/2026/04/20/enterprise-patch-remediation-benchmark-2026)

Qualys is a cloud-based vulnerability-management, compliance, and patch-management vendor. Its platform covers vulnerability scanning, the TruRisk prioritization model, and patch and remediation automation (TruRisk Eliminate). The vendor frames the platform as a shift from counting vulnerabilities to measuring risk reduction.

On this wiki Qualys appears as a source of remediation telemetry rather than as a security-AI capability. Its [[qualys-patch-remediation-benchmark-2026|Enterprise Patch & Remediation Benchmark 2026]] reports on roughly 150 million patches deployed across enterprise environments over twelve months, about 40 million of them deployed autonomously with no human in the loop.[^autonomous] That benchmark is the wiki's source for the mean time-to-remediation figure used in [[ai-era-supply-chain-hardening|AI-Era Supply Chain Hardening]].
