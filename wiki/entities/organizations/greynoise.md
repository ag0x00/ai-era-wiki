# GreyNoise

**Sources:** [GreyNoise (homepage)](https://www.greynoise.io) · [GreyNoise Labs](https://www.greynoise.io/labs)

Internet-scale honeypot and threat-intelligence vendor; operates **GreyNoise Labs** (research) and **GreyNoise Intelligence** (commercial). GreyNoise operates **Orbie**, an AI agent that runs over internet-scale honeypot data to surface emergent threats, attribute campaigns, and write detection rules.

## Orbie

[[bob-rudis|Bob Rudis]] (VP Data Science, GreyNoise Labs) and Glenn Thorpe (Sr. Director, Security Research & Detection Engineering, GreyNoise Intelligence) presented Orbie at the [[unprompted-conference-march-2026|Unprompted Conference (March 2026)]] in the talk [[detection-deception-engineering-orbie-talk|Detection & Deception Engineering in the Matrix]]. The argument: **domain-expert knowledge embedded in tooling**, not the choice of model, is what lets an LLM operate usefully over billions of network sessions.
