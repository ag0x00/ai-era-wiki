# Meta

**Sources:** [Meta (homepage)](https://about.meta.com) · [Meta AI](https://ai.meta.com)

Meta maintains the [[purple-llama|Purple Llama]] project — the wiki's main point of contact with Meta's AI-security work. Purple Llama bundles the [[llamafirewall|LlamaFirewall]] agent-guardrail framework and the [[cyberseceval|CyberSecEval]] offensive-capability benchmark suite under one purple-team banner (red-team measurement plus blue-team defense).

[[joshua-saxe|Joshua Saxe]] tech-led AI security at Meta for roughly four years — including the prompt-injection-defense work that became LlamaFirewall, for which he is the corresponding author — and presented [[measuring-agent-effectiveness-talk|Measuring Security-Agent Effectiveness]] at Unprompted March 2026.
