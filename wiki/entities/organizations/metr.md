# METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research)

Independent AI evaluation organization. The wiki's **methodological foundation** for long-task autonomy claims — the work that established the "task-completion-horizon doubles every N months" framing that [[aisi-uk|UK AISI]]'s 8-month cyber-task figure is built on.

## Notable outputs

- **"Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks"** ([metr.org blog](https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/), [arXiv:2503.14499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14499)) — Generalist task horizon doubles every ~7 months across 2019–2025; accelerated to ~4 months in 2024–2025. Methodologically transparent.
- **Time Horizon 1.1** ([metr.org/blog/2026-1-29](https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/)) — January 2026 refresh of the doubling-time analysis.
- **Common Elements of Frontier AI Safety Policies** ([metr.org/common-elements](https://metr.org/common-elements)) — Comparative view of vendor responsible-update commitments; cross-referenced from the wiki's [[agentic-ai-threat-classes-2026|Threat Classes 2026]] §Class 4 (model-version regression).

## Relevance to this corpus

[[aisi-uk|UK AISI]]'s "8-month cyber-doubling" figure is built on METR methodology. Citing UK AISI without METR is citing the conclusion without the foundation. METR is also the only independent (non-vendor, non-government) source for cross-model capability scaling.
