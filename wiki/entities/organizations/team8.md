# Team8

Israel-based venture-capital firm and company-creation foundry, founded 2014 by former Unit 8200 leadership (Nadav Zafrir and others). Focus on **cybersecurity, fintech, and data infrastructure**. In the context of this wiki, Team8 appears as the lead investor in [[lumia-security|Lumia Security]]'s December 2025 \$18M seed round — the largest single seed round in the May 2025–May 2026 agentic-AI-security window.

Team8 published [an investment thesis](https://team8.vc/the-next-generation-of-securing-ai-why-we-invested-in-lumia/) framing Lumia in the [[guardian-agent|Guardian Agent]] / [[ai-agent-management-platform|AI Agent Management Platform]] category alongside [[onyx-platform|Onyx]] and Gartner's [[ai-trism|AI TRiSM]] expansion.
