# Onyx Security

**Sources:** [Onyx Security (homepage)](https://onyx.security) · [[onyx-platform|Onyx Platform (wiki product page)]]

Onyx Security is the vendor producing the [[onyx-platform|Onyx Platform]] — marketed as a unified AI control plane spanning observability, security, governance, orchestration, and ROI tracking for enterprise AI deployments. The flagship "Onyx Guardian Agent" places the company in the [[guardian-agent|Guardian Agent]] vendor category as defined by [[gartner|Gartner]] (Feb 2026 Market Guide). Onyx adopting the analyst-defined "Guardian Agent" label rather than coining its own is a signal that the company's category positioning is still being settled — the architecturally precise label for the product is closer to **runtime protection + AI-SPM + AI gateway**, with a Gartner-flavored marketing wrapper. See [[onyx-platform|Onyx Platform §Critical assessment]] for the nomenclature note.

## Established facts

From the company's marketing site (onyx.security) as of 2026-05-03:

- The product is a five-surface platform (Observability, Security, Governance, Orchestration, ROI)
- Marketing claims 137,000+ agents secured, 593,000+ employees covered, 10M+ sessions analyzed
- Deployment options: cloud, hybrid, self-hosted; supports AWS VPC, Bedrock Gateway, custom proxy configurations
- Integration claim: 100+ pre-built integrations (AWS, GCP, Azure, OpenAI, Anthropic, browser, AI platforms, CNAPP, SASE, EDR)
