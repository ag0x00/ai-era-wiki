# Intel

**Sources:** [Intel (homepage)](https://www.intel.com) · [Intel Confidential Computing](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/security/confidential-computing.html)

Semiconductor and silicon-platform vendor (NASDAQ: INTC). In the context of this wiki, Intel appears as a contributor to two distinct agentic-AI security threads at [[unprompted-conference-march-2026|Unprompted March 2026]]:

- **Confidential AI** — Raghu Yeluri (Fellow) presented "The Advent of Confidential AI" (Day 1 / Stage 2 / 13:30): Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) based protection for inferencing data, prompts, and context, with remote attestation; two real deployments described.
- **Embodied / physical AI** — Padma Apparao presented "Kinetic Risk: Securing & Governing Physical AI in the Wild" (Day 1 / Stage 2 / 15:45): **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** models in robotics and autonomy; sensor spoofing and embodied instruction manipulation; argues NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act fall short for non-deterministic embodied AI.

Intel's two talks span the silicon end (TEE-anchored Confidential AI) and the embodied end (VLA model risk) of the agentic-AI security stack.
