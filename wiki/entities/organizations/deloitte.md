# Deloitte

**Sources:** [Deloitte CTEM / attack-surface management](https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/services/consulting-risk/services/deloitte-cyber-attack-surface-management.html) · [Claude partner blog](https://claude.com/blog/how-our-partners-are-putting-opus-to-work-for-cybersecurity)

## Identity and role

Global professional-services firm. Its [[continuous-threat-exposure-management|CTEM]] offering, built on Deloitte Ascend, runs discovery, validation, prioritization, and remediation as a single workflow — including countermeasure design when no patch exists. [[mythos|Claude Opus]] supplies code reasoning and automated stability testing.

## Relevance to This Wiki
Deloitte is a find→fix-gap entry in the [[claude-partners-opus-cybersecurity|Opus partner ecosystem]], framing remediation latency as the decisive variable — the same time-to-exploit logic the [[zero-day-clock|Zero Day Clock]] tracks. Opus's stability testing lets teams remediate in hours rather than days or weeks.

## Notable Statements / Positions
> "CTEM built on Ascend exists to help reduce decision latency in vulnerability remediation — the gap helps determine whether attackers or defenders win the window." — Adnan Amjad, partner and US Cyber leader, Deloitte
