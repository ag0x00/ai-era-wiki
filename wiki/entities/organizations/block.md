In the context of this wiki, Block appears as the **operator of an open-source AI agent** and as a contributor to **red-teaming case studies** at [[unprompted-conference-march-2026|Unprompted March 2026]].

## goose — open-source AI agent

[goose](https://block.github.io/goose/) is Block's open-source AI agent. The "Operation Pale Fire" talk at Unprompted (Day 2 / Stage 1 / 13:55, Wes Ring + Josiah Peedikayil) is a red-team retrospective on goose, framing offensive review as the proactive complement to defensive controls.
