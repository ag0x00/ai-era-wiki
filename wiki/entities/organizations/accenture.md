# Accenture

**Sources:** [Accenture (homepage)](https://www.accenture.com) · [Accenture × Anthropic newsroom](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-and-anthropic-team-to-help-organizations-secure-scale-ai-driven-cybersecurity-operations) · [Claude partner blog](https://claude.com/blog/how-our-partners-are-putting-opus-to-work-for-cybersecurity)

## Identity and role

Global professional-services and consulting firm. Its **Cyber.AI** offering is an agentic platform connecting assets, identities, threats, and controls into a single operational model, with [[mythos|Claude Opus]] reasoning across detection, prioritization, and remediation in a continuous loop.

## Relevance to This Wiki
Accenture appears in the [[claude-partners-opus-cybersecurity|Opus partner ecosystem]] as a find→fix-gap offering. It validated Cyber.AI on its own global IT before selling it: security-testing coverage rose from roughly 10% to over 80% across 1,600 applications and 500,000+ APIs, and scan turnaround fell from 3–5 days to under an hour.
