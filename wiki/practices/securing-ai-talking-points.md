# Securing AI — Talking Points

A four-point briefing outline for an AI security pitch / customer conversation.

1. "State of the industry" -- notable developments in the recent months (novel ways to attack AI). What these trend mean and what to expect in the near future.
2. Standards and Frameworks -- How regulatory bodies are catching up to the changes in threat landscape, and what is still missing from AI security standards.
3. Our approach to securing AI.
