# Cyber Defense Matrix

> [!gap] Stub
> [Sounil Yu](https://sounilyu.com)'s 5×5 Cyber Defense Matrix maps **NIST CSF functions** (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) against **five asset classes** (Devices, Applications, Networks, Data, Users). It serves as a coverage map for security tooling and a threat-modeling lens.
>
> Originally published 2016, formalized in the 2022 book *The Cyber Defense Matrix* (Wiley). [[knostic|Knostic]] and other AI security vendors are now applying the matrix to AI-era risks via published ebooks ("Rethinking Cyber Defense for the Age of AI").
>
