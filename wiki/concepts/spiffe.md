# SPIFFE / SPIRE

> [!gap] Stub
> SPIFFE (Secure Production Identity Framework for Everyone) is the open standard for cryptographic workload identity. SPIRE is the reference implementation. Together they're the substrate the [[agent-identity-architecture|AI Agent Identity Architecture]] page recommends for assigning verifiable identities to AI agents (24 mentions of SPIFFE and 14 of SPIRE across the vault).
